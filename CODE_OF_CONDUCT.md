# Code of conduct

NuOrb is a small project made by one maintainer with the help of AI tools. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or way of speaking.

## In issues, pull requests and discussions

- Be friendly and patient. Many people here are not professional developers.
- Talk about the problem, not the person. Criticism of code or ideas is fine, insults are not.
- No harassment, no discriminating or sexual remarks, no threats, no publishing other people's private data.
- Stay on topic. NuOrb is about a self-hosted voice assistant.
- Security problems go through the private reporting in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not into public issues.

## What happens otherwise

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts that break these rules. If something bothers you, contact the maintainer privately through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Anarki-LNX).

---

**Deutsch:** Alle sind willkommen. Bitte freundlich und geduldig bleiben, über die Sache reden statt über Personen, keine Belästigung, keine Diskriminierung, keine privaten Daten anderer veröffentlichen. Sicherheitsprobleme bitte privat melden (siehe [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Der Maintainer kann Beiträge entfernen und Konten sperren, die sich nicht daran halten.
